### SwiftCart Nigeria: E-commerce Management System

**Background**

SwiftCart Nigeria is a proposed e-commerce business that enables customers to purchase products online. Its operations include customer registration, product management, inventory tracking, order processing, payment recording, and delivery management.

As the business expands, it needs a structured database to manage these activities efficiently. Storing information in disconnected records can result in duplicate data, inconsistent product details, inaccurate stock levels, and difficulties tracking customer orders.

The proposed solution is a relational database management system implemented using PostgreSQL and hosted on Neon. It will organize business information into related tables and use database constraints to maintain accuracy and consistency.

**Problem statement**

SwiftCart Nigeria requires a reliable database for storing and retrieving customer, product, order, payment, inventory, and delivery information. Without a properly designed database, the business may encounter data duplication, incorrect stock records, incomplete order details, and difficulties generating sales reports.

**Aim**

The aim is to design and implement a relational database for SwiftCart Nigeria that supports its core e-commerce operations while maintaining data integrity and providing a foundation for database performance analysis and advanced database exercises.

**Objectives**

1. Identify the main entities and attributes required for e-commerce operations.
2. Design an entity-relationship model showing entities and their relationships.
3. Convert the model into a relational schema with primary keys and foreign keys.
4. Apply appropriate constraints to improve data integrity.
5. Explain how normalization reduces data duplication and update anomalies.
6. Use PostgreSQL to implement and inspect the database structure.
7. Use the same business scenario in subsequent coursework activities involving query optimization, transactions, NoSQL, and distributed or cloud databases.

**Scope**

The proposed database covers customer records, product and category information, customer orders, order items, payments, inventory, and deliveries. The project focuses on database design and implementation rather than building a complete online shopping website.

**Expected benefits**

The database should improve record organization, reduce unnecessary duplication, support order and payment tracking, make product information easier to retrieve, and provide a practical basis for analyzing database performance and reliability.
